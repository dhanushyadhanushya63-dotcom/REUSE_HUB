Our Mission & Circular Vision

Building a Greener Tamil Nadu Through Smart Reuse

ReUseHub was created to eliminate unnecessary landfill waste and make high-quality second-hand goods accessible, affordable, and trustworthy for local communities.

Affordable Choices

Buy gently-used laptops, smartphones, furniture, and books at 40% to 70% below retail prices, saving valuable household budget.

Less Waste, More Impact

Every item given a second life prevents plastic, e-waste, and carbon emissions from entering local ecosystems and landfills.

Local Neighborhood Discovery

Find products available right in your city or area (Tambaram, Adyar, T. Nagar, Gandhipuram) with clear seller pickup landmarks.

Safety First

Safe & Convenient In-Person Handover Guidelines

ReUseHub connects local buyers and sellers. Follow these best practices when coordinating your item pickup:

  • Meet in Public Well-Lit Landmarks:

    Coordinate pickups near bus terminals, metro stations, shopping malls, or apartment security gates.

  • Inspect the Item Thoroughly:

    Test electronics, check zippers, review book pages, and verify condition before finalizing the handover.

  • Use In-App Messaging:

    Keep all communication on ReUseHub to clarify product details and agree on pickup times.
